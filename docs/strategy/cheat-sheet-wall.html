[PBK] · Pullback
Retracement · Stage-2 leader
  1. Stage 2: Close > 50 EMA & 200 SMA, both sloping up, mostly above 200 SMA for 30d
  2. Structure: higher highs + higher lows over last 3 swings
  3. RS: STOCK/SPY ratio at 3-mo high & sloping up (outperforming SPY 5%+)
  4. Pullback: 3–12% off recent high, ≤15d, held above 50 EMA, touched 20 or 50 EMA
  5. Volume dry-up: pullback avg vol ≤ 70% of prior 20-day avg
  6. Not extended: distance to 20 EMA ≤ 1.5 × ATR(14)
  7. Stop viable: Entry − (Pullback low − 0.5·ATR) ≤ 2 × ATR
ALL 7 PASS → PULLBACK CANDIDATE · WRITE TRADE CARD
PBK Exit Stack (priority order)
  • Structural stop: Pullback low − 0.5·ATR
  • +2R: sell 1/3 · stop on rest → breakeven
  • Trail rest: min(20 EMA, HL − 0.5·ATR)
  • Time stop: 15 days if unrealized R < 1.0 → close
  • Regime → CHOP or worse: sell 1/2, tighten trail
  • Halal reclassified → close at next open
  • Earnings within 3 days → close before
[DC20] · Donchian 20 Breakout
